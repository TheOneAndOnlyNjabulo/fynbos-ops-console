# Supplier Invoicing Standards

## 1. Required fields

Every supplier invoice must include: the supplier's legal name and VAT registration number, a unique invoice
number, the invoice date, the purchase order (PO) number it relates to, itemized line items (description,
quantity, unit price, line total), a subtotal, VAT charged at the standard rate, and a grand total. An invoice
missing the PO number cannot be matched automatically and is routed for manual review regardless of its value.

## 2. Arithmetic consistency

The sum of all line item totals must equal the stated subtotal, and the subtotal plus VAT must equal the grand
total. Any invoice where these figures don't reconcile is flagged and held, not paid, until the supplier issues a
corrected invoice or a written explanation is obtained.

## 3. VAT requirements

VAT must be charged at the standard South African rate and shown as a separate line item, not folded into the
unit prices. An invoice from a non-VAT-registered supplier must state this explicitly rather than omitting the
VAT line silently.

## 4. Banking details

Banking details for payment must match the details already on file from the Vendor Onboarding & Compliance
process. An invoice presenting different banking details than what's on file is always flagged for manual
verification before payment, regardless of the amount - this is a common fraud vector.

## 5. Currency

All supplier invoices must be in South African Rand. Invoices in a foreign currency are routed to the finance
team for manual conversion and are never auto-approved through the standard matching process.
