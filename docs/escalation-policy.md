# Escalation Policy

## 1. When to escalate a support issue

Escalate when: a customer explicitly requests it, a response time target in the Customer Support Response
Standards has already been missed, or the issue involves a potential safety or fraud concern (for example, a
payment dispute suggesting card fraud).

## 2. Support escalation path

Frontline support agent → Customer Experience Team Lead → Customer Experience Manager. Each level has 1 business
day to acknowledge an escalation before it automatically moves to the next.

## 3. When to escalate a supplier invoice

Escalate per the Invoice Approval Thresholds & Escalation Policy for amount-based approval, or immediately,
regardless of amount, for a banking-details mismatch or suspected fraud indicator.

## 4. Escalation path for finance/invoicing issues

Accounts payable clerk → Department manager → Finance Director, matching the approval thresholds - an escalation
is not a separate process from approval routing, it's the same path triggered earlier than the amount alone would
require.

## 5. Documentation requirements

Every escalation, regardless of type, must be logged with a timestamp, the reason for escalation, and who it was
escalated to, so escalation patterns can be reviewed for recurring issues.
