# Warranty Policy

## 1. Manufacturer warranty pass-through

Most electronics and appliances carry the original manufacturer's warranty (typically 12-24 months, stated on the
product page). Fynbos Retail facilitates the claim with the manufacturer or their local agent rather than
repairing items itself.

## 2. Extended warranty option

An extended warranty can be purchased at checkout for eligible items, adding 12 or 24 months of coverage beyond
the manufacturer term. Extended warranty claims are handled directly by Fynbos Retail's customer support team, not
the manufacturer.

## 3. What a warranty claim requires

A warranty claim requires proof of purchase, the product's serial number, and a description of the fault. Cosmetic
wear and tear is not covered under any warranty tier.

## 4. Warranty claim turnaround

Manufacturer warranty claims typically take 10-15 business days, since the item is sent to the manufacturer's
service centre for assessment. Extended warranty claims handled in-house target 5-7 business days.

## 5. Relationship to Damaged & Defective Items Policy

A fault reported within 7 days of delivery is handled under the faster Damaged & Defective Items Policy, not this
warranty process. This warranty policy applies to faults that develop after that initial 7-day window, for the
remaining duration of the applicable warranty term.
