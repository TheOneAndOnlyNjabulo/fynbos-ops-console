# International Shipping Policy

## 1. Countries served

Fynbos Retail ships internationally to a limited list of neighbouring SADC countries (Namibia, Botswana,
Zimbabwe, Mozambique, Lesotho, Eswatini). Orders outside this list are not accepted at checkout.

## 2. Delivery timelines

International delivery takes 10-15 business days, considerably longer than domestic shipping, due to customs
clearance at the destination border.

## 3. Customs duties and taxes

Any import duties, customs clearance fees, or destination-country taxes are the customer's responsibility and are
not included in the order total at checkout. Fynbos Retail does not pre-pay or estimate these on the customer's
behalf.

## 4. Returns on international orders

International orders may still be returned under the standard 30-day Returns & Refunds Policy, but the customer
covers return shipping in all cases, including for change-of-mind returns - the Damaged & Defective Items
Policy's free return shipping does not extend across the border.

## 5. Restricted items

Dangerous goods, perishables, and certain electronics with battery restrictions cannot be shipped internationally
regardless of whether they are sold for domestic delivery.
