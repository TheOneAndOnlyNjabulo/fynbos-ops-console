# Payment & Billing FAQ

## 1. Accepted payment methods

Fynbos Retail accepts major debit and credit cards, instant EFT, and a buy-now-pay-later option through a
third-party partner. Cash on delivery is not offered.

## 2. Failed or duplicate charges

A failed payment that still shows as a pending charge on a customer's account typically clears automatically
within 5-7 business days, since it was never actually captured - only reserved. A genuine duplicate charge
(two separate successful charges for one order) is refunded within 3 business days once confirmed.

## 3. Buy-now-pay-later disputes

Disputes about a buy-now-pay-later instalment are handled by that third-party provider directly, not Fynbos
Retail's billing team - customer support should redirect the customer to the provider's own support channel for
instalment-specific issues, while still handling any dispute about the underlying order itself.

## 4. Invoices and tax documentation

A VAT invoice is emailed automatically on dispatch and is also available in the customer's account order history
at any time - it does not need to be requested from support.

## 5. Currency and pricing

All prices are listed and charged in South African Rand (ZAR), inclusive of VAT at the standard rate, regardless
of the customer's delivery location within South Africa.
