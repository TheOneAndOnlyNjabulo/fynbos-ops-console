# Complaints & Dispute Resolution Process

## 1. Scope

This process covers complaints not tied to a single resolvable order issue - for example, dissatisfaction with
how a previous support interaction was handled, or a complaint about a courier's conduct. Order-specific issues
(returns, damaged items, shipping delays) are handled under their own dedicated policies first.

## 2. Submission channels

Complaints may be submitted via the support inbox, the website's contact form, or by phone.

## 3. Resolution stages

Stage 1: frontline resolution by customer support within 2 business days. Stage 2, if unresolved: review by a
Customer Experience Team Lead within 5 business days. Stage 3, if still unresolved: a formal written response from
management within 10 business days.

## 4. Timeline commitments

Each stage is measured from when the complaint is formally logged, not from the original incident date. The
customer is notified in writing whenever a complaint moves to the next stage.

## 5. External escalation

A customer who remains unsatisfied after Stage 3 may refer the matter to the National Consumer Commission. This
right exists regardless of Fynbos Retail's internal process and must never be withheld or delayed as a condition
of internal resolution.
