# Loyalty & Rewards Program

## 1. Earning points

Members earn 1 point per R10 spent (excluding delivery fees and gift card purchases). Points are credited once an
order is delivered, not at the time of purchase, so a returned order's points are reversed.

## 2. Membership tiers

Bronze (default), Silver (from R5,000 spent in a rolling 12 months), and Gold (from R15,000 spent in a rolling 12
months). Silver members get free standard shipping; Gold members get free standard shipping plus early access to
sales.

## 3. Redeeming points

Points can be redeemed in blocks of 100 points for R10 off an order, up to a maximum of 50% of the order value
per transaction. Points cannot be redeemed for cash or combined with a promotional voucher on the same order.

## 4. Points expiry

Points expire after 12 months of account inactivity (no purchase, login, or points redemption). An active account
never loses points regardless of how long individual point batches have been held.

## 5. Tier downgrades

Tier status is reviewed annually based on the trailing 12 months of spend; a member who no longer meets a tier's
threshold is moved down at the annual review, not immediately when spend drops.
