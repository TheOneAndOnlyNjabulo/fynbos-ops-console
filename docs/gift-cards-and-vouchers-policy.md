# Gift Cards & Vouchers Policy

## 1. Validity period

Gift cards are valid for 3 years from the date of purchase. An expired gift card balance cannot be reinstated or
extended.

## 2. Non-refundable for cash

Gift cards cannot be redeemed for cash, in full or in part, except where required by law. A gift card used to pay
for an order that is later refunded is refunded back onto a gift card, not to a bank account or original card.

## 3. Combining with other payment methods

A gift card can be combined with a card or EFT payment to cover the remainder of an order, but not combined with
another gift card and a promotional voucher in the same transaction - only one voucher code per order.

## 4. Lost or stolen gift card codes

A lost digital gift card code can be reissued to the original purchaser's email address on request, provided the
balance has not already been spent. Physical gift cards reported lost or stolen cannot be reissued or have their
balance recovered.

## 5. Promotional vouchers versus gift cards

Promotional vouchers (percentage-off or Rand-off codes issued for marketing campaigns) are distinct from gift
cards: vouchers typically expire within 30-90 days, apply only to specific product categories, and have no
resale or cash value.
