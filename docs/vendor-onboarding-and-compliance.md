# Vendor Onboarding & Compliance

## 1. Onboarding requirements

Before a new supplier can be paid, they must provide: company registration documents, a valid VAT registration
number (if VAT-registered), a tax clearance certificate, and verified banking details.

## 2. Banking detail verification

Banking details are verified via a small test deposit before being marked active in the vendor system. A change
to a previously verified supplier's banking details triggers the same verification process again before any
further payment is released - this is what the banking-details check in the Supplier Invoicing Standards relies
on being current.

## 3. Ongoing compliance checks

Tax clearance certificates are re-verified annually. A supplier with an expired tax clearance certificate is
suspended from new purchase orders until it is renewed, though existing open POs are still honoured.

## 4. Conflict of interest disclosure

Any Fynbos Retail employee with a personal or financial relationship to a supplier must disclose it before that
supplier is onboarded or before any purchase order is raised with them.

## 5. Offboarding

A supplier no longer in use is marked inactive rather than deleted, so historical invoices and purchase orders
remain auditable.
