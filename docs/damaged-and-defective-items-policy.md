# Damaged & Defective Items Policy

## 1. Reporting window

A damaged or defective item must be reported within 7 days of delivery, with photos of the damage or fault
attached. This is separate from and shorter than the 30-day standard return window, since prompt reporting makes
it possible to distinguish delivery damage from later misuse.

## 2. Resolution options

For a confirmed damaged or defective item, the customer chooses between a free replacement (subject to stock) or
a full refund. Both options are available regardless of which non-returnable category the item might otherwise
fall under per the Returns & Refunds Policy - damage/defect claims are never blocked by the final-sale exclusion
list.

## 3. Return shipping cost

Fynbos Retail covers return shipping for a confirmed damaged or defective item - the customer is never charged to
send back a faulty product. A prepaid shipping label is issued once the claim is approved.

## 4. Assessment before approval

Items above R2,000 in value require photo assessment by the supplier or a technician before a replacement or
refund is approved, which can add 2-3 business days to the process. Items below that threshold are approved
directly from customer-submitted photos.

## 5. Damage caused by misuse

Damage clearly caused by misuse (drops, exposure to liquid on a non-waterproof item, unauthorized repair attempts)
is not covered under this policy and is instead assessed under the item's Warranty Policy terms, if applicable.
