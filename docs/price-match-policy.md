# Price Match Policy

## 1. Eligibility window

A price match request must be submitted within 7 days of purchase. Requests submitted after this window are
declined regardless of the price difference found.

## 2. Qualifying competitors

Only listed South African online retailers with their own stock and checkout (not third-party marketplace
listings, auction sites, or grey-import sellers) qualify as a price match reference.

## 3. Identical item requirement

The competitor's price must be for the exact same brand, model, colour, and specification - a similar or
"equivalent" item from another brand does not qualify.

## 4. What is matched

Fynbos Retail refunds the difference between the price paid and the lower competitor price; it does not match a
competitor's shipping cost or bundle promotions (e.g. "buy one get one free").

## 5. Exclusions

Clearance, liquidation, and flash-sale pricing are excluded from price matching, since these are time-limited
promotions rather than a competitor's standard price.
