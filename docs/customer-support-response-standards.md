# Customer Support Response Standards

## 1. Required elements of every reply

Every customer support reply must include: acknowledgement of the specific issue raised, a clear next step or
resolution, any timeline that applies (per the relevant policy), and the order or ticket reference number.

## 2. Response time targets

Order issues (status, delivery delay) get a reply within 4 business hours. Returns, refunds, and billing issues
get a reply within 1 business day. General questions get a reply within 2 business days.

## 3. Tone guidelines

Replies should be warm, plain-language, and specific to the customer's actual situation - not a generic
copy-paste of the relevant policy. Acknowledge inconvenience without over-apologising or admitting fault before
the facts are confirmed.

## 4. Prohibited claims

Do not promise a specific delivery date the courier has not confirmed, and do not state a refund has been
processed before it has actually been submitted to the payment gateway. A drafted reply that makes either claim
must be corrected before it is sent.

## 5. Citing policy accurately

Any specific timeline, threshold, or eligibility rule stated to a customer must match the actual current policy
document - do not round a policy number for simplicity (e.g. do not say "about a week" when the policy states a
specific business-day figure), since customers hold the company to what support tells them.
