# Invoice Approval Thresholds & Escalation Policy

## 1. Auto-approval threshold

An invoice under R5,000 that matches its purchase order and passes the arithmetic and banking-details checks in
the Supplier Invoicing Standards may be auto-approved for payment without manual sign-off.

## 2. Manager approval threshold

An invoice between R5,000 and R50,000 requires sign-off from a department manager before payment, even if it
otherwise passes every automated check.

## 3. Finance director approval threshold

An invoice over R50,000 requires sign-off from the Finance Director, regardless of whether it matches its
purchase order.

## 4. Mandatory escalation regardless of amount

Any invoice with a banking-details mismatch, a missing PO number, or an arithmetic inconsistency is escalated for
manual review regardless of its value - these conditions override the amount-based thresholds above, since they
indicate a data or fraud risk rather than a routine approval-level decision.

## 5. Escalation turnaround

Manager-level approvals are expected within 2 business days of being flagged; Finance Director approvals within 5
business days. An invoice awaiting approval past these windows should be followed up rather than left pending
indefinitely.
