# POPIA Data Privacy Notice

## 1. Purpose and scope

This notice explains how Fynbos Retail collects, uses, and protects personal information in connection with
online orders and customer support, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

## 2. Personal information collected

Order and account data (name, delivery address, contact details, order history), payment metadata (last 4 digits
of card, payment method type - never full card numbers, which are handled solely by the payment gateway), and the
content of support interactions.

## 3. Lawful basis and purpose of processing

Personal information is processed to fulfil orders, provide customer support, and for account and loyalty program
management. Marketing communications require separate, explicit opt-in consent.

## 4. Data sharing with couriers and suppliers

Only the minimum information needed to deliver an order (name, address, contact number) is shared with the
courier. Suppliers handling a warranty claim receive the product serial number and fault description, not the
customer's full order history.

## 5. Retention period

Order records are retained for 5 years to meet tax and consumer protection recordkeeping requirements, after
which personal identifiers are anonymised.

## 6. Customer rights

A customer may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information, subject to Fynbos
Retail's lawful retention obligations. Requests are directed to the Information Officer via customer support.
