# Returns & Refunds Policy

## 1. Standard return window

Most items may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, provided the item is unused, in its
original packaging, and accompanied by proof of purchase. The 30-day window is calculated from the delivery date
recorded in the courier's system, not the order date.

## 2. Non-returnable items

The following are final sale and cannot be returned unless faulty: opened hygiene and personal care items,
underwear and swimwear, perishable goods, gift cards, and custom or made-to-order items. This exclusion list is
independent of the Damaged & Defective Items Policy, which covers faulty items regardless of category.

## 3. Refund processing time

Once a returned item is received at the warehouse and inspected, refunds are processed within 5-7 business days
back to the original payment method. Refunds are never issued before the item is physically received, even if a
return has been approved.

## 4. Return shipping cost

For a standard change-of-mind return, the customer covers return shipping. For a faulty or incorrectly shipped
item, Fynbos Retail covers return shipping - see the Damaged & Defective Items Policy for that process.

## 5. Partial refunds

A partial refund (rather than a full refund) applies when an item is returned without its original packaging or
accessories, or shows signs of use beyond inspection - typically 80% of the item's value, at the discretion of
the warehouse inspection team.
