# Shipping & Delivery Policy

## 1. Standard delivery

Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days within South Africa's major metros, and 5-7 business days to outlying
areas. Orders are dispatched within 1 business day of payment confirmation.

## 2. Express delivery

Express delivery is available at checkout for an additional fee and takes 1-2 business days within major metros.
Express orders placed after 12:00 are dispatched the next business day.

## 3. Free shipping threshold

Orders over R500 (after discounts, before any express delivery fee) qualify for free standard delivery. Orders
under R500 incur a flat R60 standard delivery fee.

## 4. Delivery delays

If a delivery is running more than 2 business days behind its original estimate, the customer should be
proactively notified with a revised date rather than left to ask. Delays caused by the courier (not Fynbos
Retail) do not entitle the customer to a delivery fee refund, but do entitle them to real-time tracking updates on
request.

## 5. Failed delivery attempts

The courier will attempt delivery twice. After two failed attempts, the parcel is held at the nearest depot for 5
business days before being returned to Fynbos Retail's warehouse, at which point the order is treated as a
customer-initiated cancellation for refund purposes.
